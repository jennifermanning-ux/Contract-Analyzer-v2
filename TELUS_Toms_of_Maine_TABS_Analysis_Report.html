📋 Executive Summary

Deal Overview: This Statement of Work between Tom's of Maine, Inc. and TABS Group, Inc. outlines a 6-month engagement for in-house analytic support services, effective September 14, 2015. The agreement is governed by an underlying Master Services Agreement dated August 14, 2015. The primary scope involves refreshing POS reports, providing strategic recommendations, and delivering ad hoc sales analysis for the Customer and Shopper Marketing Team.

Financial Mechanics: The commercial model is a fixed monthly fee of $5,000 for a six-month test period, totaling $30,000 in committed revenue. Additional office days beyond the two included per month are billable at $500 per day. Travel expenses are treated as pass-through costs, reimbursable by the customer subject to Colgate-Palmolive's Travel and Expense Policy.

Delivery and Risk: Delivery relies on a named Key Personnel resource, Daniel Kresina, who will provide on-site support every other week and remote support of 30-40 hours per month. A critical risk flag exists regarding entity alignment; the contracting entity 'TABS Group, Inc.' does not match the standard TAC entity guidance for Consumer Goods (USA), necessitating Tax and Legal review. No cross-border complexities were detected as both parties appear to be US-based.

Strategic Verdict: The agreement represents a short-term tactical engagement with clear deliverables and ownership rights assigned to the customer. While the financial value is low, the 'test' nature of the term suggests potential for future expansion if the analytic support proves valuable. Immediate action is required to validate the contracting entity alignment to ensure compliance with corporate structure.
