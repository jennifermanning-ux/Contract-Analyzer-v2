📋 Executive Summary

Deal Overview: This Order Form and Statement of Work outlines a professional services engagement for The Kraft Heinz Food Company to manage the splitting of their existing TPM Enterprise instance into two new entities: Global Taste Elevation Co. (GTE) and North American Grocery Co. (NAG). The project is scheduled to commence on February 16, 2026, with a targeted Go-Live date of July 13, 2026, followed by four weeks of Hypercare ending August 10, 2026.

Financial Mechanics: The total contract value is a Fixed Fee of $575,000.00 USD, exclusive of taxes. Billing is tied to three milestones: Contract Execution ($191,666.67), Completion of Test Environment Creation ($191,666.67), and Go Live ($191,666.66). Payment terms are Net 30 days. Any resource usage exceeding the allocated weekly percentages (25% for PM, 50% for Consultant/Engineer) will be billed on a Time and Materials basis at $275.00 per hour.

Delivery and Risk: The scope involves creating two new environments for NAG while retaining GTE on the existing instance. TELUS is responsible for environment setup, interface cloning, and testing support, while data conversion, training, and code updates are explicitly out of scope. Operational risk is mitigated by strict dependency clauses; Customer delays exceeding 10 days trigger a penalty of $2,000 USD per resource per day.

Strategic Verdict: The agreement is governed by a 2014 Master Services Agreement. The entities are aligned with TELUS Agriculture & Consumer Goods (US) Inc. contracting with a US-based customer entity. There are no significant cross-border or regulatory red flags detected in the provided text. The deal represents a standard professional services enhancement for an existing enterprise customer.
