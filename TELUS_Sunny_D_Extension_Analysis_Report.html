📎 Extension Agreement

This document is an Extension Agreement to the Verisync Subscription Agreement originally dated October 1, 2004. It extends the term and modifies pricing for a 3-year period.

⚠ HIGH PRIORITY: Multiple Risk Flags Detected

Entity Misalignment: MEI Computer Technology Group Inc. does not align with TAC Contracting Entity Guidance.
Cross-Border Transaction: Canada-based entity contracting with USA customer requires Tax and Legal review.

📋 Executive Summary

Deal Overview: This Extension Agreement renews the Verisync Subscription Agreement (originally dated October 1, 2004) between Sunny Delight Beverages Co. and MEI Computer Technology Group Inc. The extension covers a 3-year term effective from September 30, 2011, through September 29, 2014. The agreement is executed by both parties, with Lorne Schwartz, CEO signing for MEI Computer Technology Group Inc.

Financial Mechanics: The total contract value for the 3-year extension is approximately $511,704, structured as $170,568 per year. The monthly rate is set at $14,214.00, which includes a stated 3.0% subscription increase applied to all three years of the renewal. Billing is described as 'paid monthly on the Year 1 anniversary of the contract signature' for each respective subscription year, a phrasing that may require operational clarification.

Delivery and Risk: The primary risk identified is entity misalignment and cross-border complexity. The contracting entity, MEI Computer Technology Group Inc. (Montreal, Quebec), does not match the standard TAC entity guidance, and the customer is located in Ohio, USA. This cross-border structure triggers a mandatory High Risk flag requiring Tax and Legal engagement. No specific service level agreements (SLAs) or support terms were found in this extension document.

Strategic Verdict: The agreement effectively locks in recurring revenue for a legacy product (Verisync) for three years with a built-in price uplift. However, the non-standard contracting entity and cross-border nature of the deal necessitate immediate review by Tax and Legal to ensure compliance and proper revenue recognition. All other terms of the original 2004 agreement remain in full force.
