📋 Executive Summary

Deal Overview: This Amendment #2 to SOW 105 between TKXS, LLC and BASF Corporation extends the term of SalesX Customer Experience Support through February 4, 2022. The agreement renews a dedicated team of two Client Experience Specialists and expands the scope to include portal administration and LMX troubleshooting. The effective date is January 22, 2021.

Financial Mechanics: The total contract value for the extended term is $334,956, billed monthly at $27,913. Fees cover Program/Systems Management ($20,000), Dedicated Support ($309,706), and Technology Licenses ($5,250). Out-of-pocket expenses are treated as pass-through costs. The agreement notes a potential 5% discount for future multi-year commitments in years 2 and 3.

Delivery and Risk: Services are provided Monday through Friday, 8 am – 5 pm EST. The scope includes Tier 1 and Tier 2 support, with provisions for additional value-added work like FAQ writing during non-peak times. Operational risks include potential cost increases if call volumes exceed 2020-2021 levels, which would require a Change Management Order.

Strategic Verdict: The contracting entity TKXS, LLC does not align with the standard TAC Contracting Entity Guidance tables, triggering a high-risk flag for entity misalignment. Renewal requires a 90-day written notice from BASF. The deal structure relies on the Master Subscription and Services Agreement dated May 30, 2019.
