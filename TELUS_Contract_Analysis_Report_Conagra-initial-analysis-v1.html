📋 Executive Summary

This Statement of Work outlines a $698,000 fixed-fee engagement between TELUS Agriculture & Consumer Goods (US) Inc. and Conagra Brands, Inc. to integrate TELUS TPM Enterprise with SAP S/4HANA for Conagra's US business unit. The project runs from a March 2026 kickoff through Hypercare completion on June 30, 2027, with a blackout period in early 2027. The agreement relies on a master agreement (MSSA) dated July 1, 2009.

The total contract value is $698,000, billed across six defined milestones including Test Environment Build (10%), Unit Testing (20%), Test Program Load (15%), UAT Sign Off (30%), Cutover & Go Live (15%), and Hypercare Completion (10%). Invoicing occurs upon written customer acceptance of each milestone. Additional support during the Memorial Day 2027 cutover weekend is billable on a Time & Materials basis at rates of $300-$450 per hour.

Scope is strictly limited to the US market, with Canada explicitly excluded. Technical delivery includes pricing, contract management workflows, and analytics, alongside specific integrations with SAP S/4HANA and Tibersoft. TELUS is responsible for train-the-trainer sessions, while the customer retains responsibility for end-user training and UAT execution. A blackout period is scheduled for January and February 2027 where project work pauses.

The deal represents a significant implementation project within the Consumer Goods vertical, reinforcing the customer's reliance on TELUS solutions for trade promotion management. The explicit exclusion of Canada and specific functional areas suggests clear boundaries for the current engagement while highlighting potential future expansion opportunities. Operational alignment appears robust with defined governance, though the reliance on a 2009 master agreement suggests a long-standing relationship.
